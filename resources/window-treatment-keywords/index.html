Window Treatment Keywords

Start your list of window treatment keywords with what you sell and where you install it. A homeowner searching for plantation shutters in their city needs a different page from someone comparing roller and cellular shades.

Match the search to the page

  • Product pages

    Give the products you sell their own pages when a shopper needs different information to choose. A motorized shades page can explain controls, power options and what you install.

  • Location pages

    If you have a location that serves a distinct area, use its page to give shoppers the local details they need. Include the products available there and the way to book with that team.

  • Buying guides

    Use a guide to answer questions that need more room, such as which shades work for patio doors. Link from the answer to the relevant product pages so a reader can see what you offer.

Four searches you can build around

Use the place names your customers actually use. These examples show what each searcher needs to find.

Plantation shutters in [city]
Show shutter installations, explain materials and options, and make it clear you serve that city.
Motorized shades near me
Explain the motors and controls you carry and where shoppers can book a consultation.
Roller shades vs. cellular shades
Help the reader compare how the two options look and how they handle light and privacy.
[Store name] hours
Send shoppers to a location page with current hours, contact information and any appointment details.

Give each search a place to land

  • Group terms that mean the same thing

    Put variations together when they point to the same product or service. Give a search a separate page when the visitor needs a different answer.

  • Check what already gets clicks

    In Google Search Console, look at the queries that bring people to a page. If two pages keep appearing for the same buying search, read them side by side and decide which gives the shopper the better answer.
