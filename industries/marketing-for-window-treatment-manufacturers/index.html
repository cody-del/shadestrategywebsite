Give your reps a site they can share

Your site is a reference for the retailers you work with. When they have a product question, your reps can send them straight to the page that answers it.

Keep the brand consistent

Use the same collection names, product photos, and descriptions on your site and in your ads as you do in your catalog. Retailers can tell they’re looking at the same product wherever they find it.

Put the details on the collection page

When a retailer wants to check a spec or compare options, they should be able to do it from the collection page. That gives them one place to come back to as they work through the product with a customer.
