Websites for Window Treatment Manufacturers

We build websites for window treatment manufacturers that give retailers a place to look up your brand and products after a conversation with a rep. The site puts collections, photos and product information where buyers can find and share them.

Give your reps a page worth sending

  • Make collections easy to browse

    Show what you manufacture and group the lines the way your reps talk about them. Give each collection its own page so a rep can send a retailer straight to it.

  • Put the specs by the product

    Keep options, finishes, operating systems and available downloads beside the relevant photos. Retailers can check the details while deciding what belongs in their assortment.

  • Keep the relationship with your team

    Make it clear how a retailer can follow up with the right person. We can organize rep contacts, territories or a product inquiry around the way your team works.

Show us what your reps share today

Bring a catalog, a product sheet or your current website. We’ll look at what a retailer needs to find online after talking to your team.
