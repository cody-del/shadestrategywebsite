Multi-Location Window Treatment Websites

We build multi-location window treatment websites that let shoppers find the team serving their address. Each location gets its own information and booking path while your product pages and brand live on one site.

Book a strategy call ↗ See the location setup →

Give every location a clear role

  • Show the right local details

    Put the phone number, hours, service area and appointment information on each location page. Add local photos and project examples where you have them.

  • Keep product pages together

    Use one page for a product line you sell across locations. Give shoppers a clear next step to find their local team from that page.

  • Route each inquiry

    Decide who should receive a call or form from each market. We map those routes while planning the site so an inquiry goes to the team that can follow up.

Walk us through your locations

Tell us where your teams work, what they share and who handles new inquiries. We’ll use that to map the site.
