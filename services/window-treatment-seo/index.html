Where homeowners can find you on Google

A search can show several of these at once. The order can change based on what the homeowner searches for and where they are.

  • Google Ads

    Search ads can appear near the top of the page. With managed Google Ads, we target the products you install in the towns where you want more jobs.

  • AI Overviews

    For some searches, Google shows a summary with links to supporting pages. A page that answers a real buying question, such as whether existing shades can be motorized, can be one of those sources.

  • Google Maps

    For nearby shoppers, we work toward a spot in the top three map results. We review how your Google Business Profile represents what you sell and where you work.

  • Organic results

    Distance can make it harder to show up on the map in towns farther out. A page about the product you install and the area you serve gives shoppers another way to find you.

What does a shopper see when they click?

If motorized shades get one line on your Products page, there’s room to show people what you install and how they can book a measure.

Before

A general Products page

“We offer blinds, shades, shutters, and motorization.”

The shopper still has to find out which shades you motorize and whether you install in their town.

After

A page about motorized shades

  • Photos from an installation you did nearby.
  • The motor and control options you install.
  • The towns you serve and how to book an in-home measure.
