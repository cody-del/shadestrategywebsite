Answers to what matters most for your manufacturers marketing strategy

How is Shade Strategy different from other marketing agencies?

We exclusively serve the window treatment industry. Our founder operated a window treatment business for over a decade, so we understand dealer relationships, manufacturing challenges, and wholesale dynamics from experience—not theory.

What if I already have a website?

We can work with your existing site, completely rebuild it, or create a hybrid approach. We'll audit what you have and recommend the most cost-effective path forward.

How much does this cost?

Investment varies based on market size, competition, and which services you need. Most retailers invest $1,500-5,000/month for comprehensive marketing (SEO + Ads + Automation). We can create a custom plan for your budget and goals.

Can you help with both B2B (dealer recruitment) and B2C (helping our dealers market to consumers)?

Absolutely. We can help you attract dealers while also providing white-label marketing solutions you can offer to your dealer network.

How long does it take to see results?

SEO typically shows meaningful results in 3-6 months. Automation and website improvements deliver immediate efficiency gains. Advertising and re-activation campaigns can generate leads within weeks.

Do you require long-term contracts?

We offer both project-based and ongoing retainer arrangements. Most manufacturers see the best results with ongoing partnerships, but we're flexible based on your needs.

Do you integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. We're experienced with most CRM platforms, ERPs, and business management systems common in wholesale distribution.

Do you work with manufacturers of all sizes?

Yes. Whether you're a regional supplier looking to expand or an established manufacturer competing against national brands, we tailor our services to your specific situation and goals.
