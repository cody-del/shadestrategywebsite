The Problem With Traditional Google Ads

Broad, Low-Intent Traffic

Generic keyword targeting attracts users who are researching or browsing rather than actively looking to buy.

Inefficient Location Targeting

Budgets are often spread evenly instead of focused on the most profitable service areas.

Mismatched Search Intent

Educational and DIY searches dilute performance when not properly filtered out.

Mobile Experience Gaps

Most searches happen on mobile, yet many campaigns still send traffic to desktop-oriented pages.
