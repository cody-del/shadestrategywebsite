What's the difference between general SEO and window covering SEO?

General SEO follows a template. Window covering SEO requires understanding the nuances that make this industry unique.Product complexity: Most SEO agencies wouldn't know to create separate content strategies for plantation shutters, wood blinds, faux wood blinds, aluminum blinds, roller shades, cellular shades, Roman shades, and motorized options—each with different customer demographics, price points, and search behaviors. We do, because we've sold all of them.Local search dynamics: Window covering businesses live or die by local SEO. Customers rarely travel more than 30 minutes for in-home consultations. General agencies optimize for city-level keywords; we optimize for neighborhood-level searches and create location-specific content that dominates local markets.Customer education: The window covering buying cycle is long and research-intensive. Generic SEO focuses on transactional keywords, but we build content strategies around educational queries like "how long do plantation shutters last" or "difference between honeycomb and cellular shades" that capture customers early in their journey.Competitive landscape: We know who your real competitors are—not just local businesses, but national big-box stores, and online retailers. We position you strategically against each segment.Answer Engine Optimization: We're pioneering AEO specifically for window covering companies, ensuring your business gets recommended by AI search engines when customers ask conversational questions about window covering solutions. Window covering SEO isn't about getting more traffic—it's about getting the right traffic that converts into profitable consultations and installations.
