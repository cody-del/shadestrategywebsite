Complete B2B Marketing Services for Manufacturers & Wholesalers

Dealer Recruitment & Lead Generation

Expanding your dealer network requires systematic outreach to qualified prospects in target markets. We help you identify, attract, and convert independent retailers who match your ideal dealer profile.

What we do:

  • Identify target markets and create prospect lists of quality independent retailers
  • Develop territory-specific outreach campaigns highlighting your competitive advantages
  • Create dealer recruitment landing pages optimized for conversion
  • Launch LinkedIn campaigns targeting window treatment retail owners and managers
  • Implement email nurture sequences that educate prospects over 6-12 months
  • Track dealer inquiry quality and sales team follow-up effectiveness
  • Develop case studies from successful dealer partnerships to use in recruitment
  • Create compelling "Why Partner With Us" materials that differentiate your offering

Results you'll see: Consistent flow of qualified dealer inquiries (10-20 monthly in target markets), higher-quality prospects who understand your value proposition, shorter sales cycles because marketing pre-educates prospects.

Dealer Support & Enablement

What we do:

  • Develop consumer-facing content (buying guides, product comparisons) dealers can share
  • Design social media templates dealers can post with one click

Results you'll see: Higher dealer satisfaction and retention, increased product visibility in dealer showrooms, more dealers actively promoting your brand online, improved co-op program utilization rates (from 20-30% to 50-70%).

Results you'll see: 2-3x more booth traffic than typical attendance, higher-quality conversations with pre-qualified prospects, 40-60% of leads progressing to serious dealer discussions within 90 days, measurable ROI on show investment.

Digital Presence & Search Visibility

When dealers and designers research suppliers, your brand needs to appear in search results with compelling information that differentiates your offering.

What we do:

  • Rebuild website with clear product information, dealer resources, and specification materials
  • Optimize for B2B search terms: "[product type] manufacturer," "wholesale window treatments," "to-the-trade [product]"
  • Create comprehensive product pages with technical specifications designers need
  • Develop resource libraries (installation guides, CAD drawings, sustainability certifications)
  • Implement dealer portal with secure access to pricing, ordering, and marketing materials
  • Build separate designer portal with specification resources and sample programs
  • Optimize page speed and mobile experience (many designers research on tablets)
  • Create location pages for regional showroom or distribution center locations

Results you'll see: Page one rankings for product and industry terms within 6-9 months, increased organic traffic from qualified prospects, reduced burden on sales team answering basic questions, easier dealer recruitment as prospects research your brand.

The Challenges Facing Manufacturers and Wholesalers Today

Running a window treatment manufacturing or wholesale business means navigating complex B2B relationships in an increasingly competitive market. Here's what you're up against:

Recruiting and retaining quality dealers is harder than ever. You're competing against established brands with decades of dealer relationships, bigger co-op budgets, and national recognition. Independent retailers have more supplier options than ever before. Designers are discovering new brands through Instagram and industry publications. Your sales team spends months cultivating relationships, only to lose dealers to competitors offering better marketing support or more attractive terms.

Your dealers aren't selling your products effectively. You've invested in beautiful catalogs, sample programs, and training materials – but dealers aren't using them. They're not promoting your brand on their websites. They're not mentioning your products in consultations. When homeowners walk into their showroom, your samples sit in the back while competitors' products get featured. Your products are being treated as commodity options rather than premium solutions worth promoting.

Brand awareness lags behind the big players. Hunter Douglas, Graber, and other major brands have mind share with both dealers and designers. When retailers search for new suppliers or designers specify products, those established names come up first. You're offering comparable or superior products, but without brand recognition, you're constantly fighting an uphill battle just to get consideration.

Digital presence is weak or nonexistent. Your website looks like it was built in 2015. You're barely visible in search results for industry terms. Dealers can't find product information easily. Designers looking for specification materials give up and choose brands with better online resources. Your competitors are showing up on Instagram, LinkedIn, and industry publications while you're invisible to new prospects.

Sound familiar? Most manufacturers and wholesalers we work with are frustrated because they've invested heavily in product development, quality, and customer service – but their marketing doesn't communicate that value effectively to the trade.
