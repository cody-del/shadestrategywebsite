FAQs

Can't I just use a DIY website builder?

You can. But those platforms are usually slow, limited in functionality, and harder to optimize for SEO. Fine for a hobby, not ideal for a serious business competing in local search.

What if I want to update my own content?

We build sites that give you full control to update content, add pages, and make changes through an easy-to-use content management system. No technical knowledge required.

Do you handle hosting?

Yes. We use fast, reliable hosting optimized for performance and security. Included in ongoing maintenance.

What about maintenance and updates?

Optional monthly maintenance includes updates, backups, security monitoring, and minor content changes. Most clients opt for this because it's easier than managing it themselves.

Can you redesign my existing site?

Sometimes yes, sometimes it's better to rebuild from scratch with modern code. We'll assess your current site and recommend the best approach.
