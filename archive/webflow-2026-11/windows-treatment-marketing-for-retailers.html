Answers to what matters most for your retail marketing strategy

How is Shade Strategy different from other marketing agencies?

We exclusivelyserve the window treatment and window covering industry. Our founder operated a windowtreatment retail business for over a decade, so we understand the customer journey, salesprocess, and industry challenges from experience—not theory. We know what actually works toconvert leads into customers.

What if I already have a website?

We can optimize your existing website or build a new one, depending on its current state. We'll also integrate it with the right window treatment business software (CRM, automation tools) to maximize efficiency. We'll audit what you have and recommend the most cost-effective approach.

How much does this cost?

Investment varies based on market size, competition, and which services you need. Most retailers invest $1,500-5,000/month for comprehensive marketing (SEO + Ads + Automation). We can create a custom plan for your budget and goals.

Will I have to manage any of this myself?

No. We handle everything from strategy to execution to reporting. You'll get monthly updates on performance, but the day-to-day work is onus. Your job is to take consultations and close sales.

How long does it take to see results?

Google Ads and automation deliver immediate results. Local SEO typically shows meaningful rankings and traffic increases within 3-6 months.Review generation starts producing results within weeks.

Do you require long-term contracts?

We offer both project-based work and month-to-month retainers. Most retailers see the best results with ongoing partnerships, but we're flexible based on your needs and budget.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings?

No legitimate agency can guarantee specific rankings (Google's algorithm is too complex). However, we can show you our track record of getting retailers to the first page for multiple keywords within 3-6 months.

What if I'm in a small market?

Perfect! Smaller markets are actually easier to dominate because there's less competition. You can often achieve top rankings faster and at lower cost than larger metro areas.
